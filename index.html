About Mohora

Mohora is a local multi-vendor marketplace platform that connects neighbourhood shop owners, small businesses and customers in one place.

Mohora was created because local commerce deserves the same digital tools that large companies use. Many local shop owners work with manual records, limited reach and no easy way to be found online.

Our mission: Empower local businesses through technology.

Problems local shops face

  • Limited visibility beyond the neighbourhood
  • Manual handling of orders and inventory
  • No simple way to manage payments and delivery
  • High effort to build an online presence

How Mohora helps

Sellers get a digital storefront and management tools. Customers discover nearby businesses and order through a connected local marketplace.

FAQ

What is Mohora?

Mohora is a local multi-vendor marketplace platform connecting shop owners, small businesses and customers.

How does Mohora work?

Sellers register and create storefronts. Customers discover nearby businesses. Orders and fulfilment are managed through Mohora.

Who can register as a seller?

Local shop owners, small businesses and retailers can apply through the Seller Portal.

How can I join Mohora as a customer?

Choose Get Started, then Customer Portal, and register.

How do sellers manage their shops?

Sellers use the Seller Portal to manage their shop, products, inventory, orders and payouts.

Is Mohora available in my city?

Availability depends on local seller coverage. Contact us to ask about your city.

How can I contact support?

Use the contact form below and choose Customer Support or Seller Support.
